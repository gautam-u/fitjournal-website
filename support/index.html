How can we help?

Contact gautam.ux@gmail.com with the app version, device model, iOS version and a short description of what happened. Please leave out private records, payment details and anything you do not want to share.

Common questions

Will variety silently replace my plan?

Saved base and variety workouts let you control A/B alternation. Review changes before applying them, and use the app’s save or update option to choose what persists.

Is Apple Intelligence required?

No. Manual plans, exercise browsing and workout logging remain available. Supported text interpretation requires a compatible device and an available on-device model.

Does Gain prescribe an exercise for my body measurements?

The app can preserve optional training context in a plan. It does not diagnose joint mechanics or guarantee that a movement suits your body. Review exercise choices and use appropriate professional guidance when needed.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Manage or cancel an auto-renewing subscription in your Apple Account settings. The App Store handles purchases and refund requests.
